## How to agree

1. Submit your first Pull Request to the Project.
2. Read the terms above.
3. Comment on your Pull Request (or open it) one of these exact signals:
   - `I agree to the [Astrivya CLA](CLA.md) for this and all future
     contributions.`
   - If you're contributing on behalf of an entity instead, `I agree to the
     [Astrivya CLA] as an authorized signer of <Entity Name>.`
4. A maintainer will label the PR `cla-signed` after verification.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Does this CLA cover all future contributions?** Yes — once you accept this
CLA, it covers this Contribution and every Contribution you make later as long
as you remain a contributor.

**Q: Do I give up my copyright?** No — you retain copyright for your
Contributions; the point is you grant us a license under the other Project
Licenses, not double licensing for the open-source builds.

**Q: The Project is Apache-2.0. Why a CLA?** Because parts of Astrivya are
dual-licensed (Apache-2.0 OSS builds and FSL-1.1 enterprise/cloud). A CLA lets
us re-license or dual-license your Contribution without having to email you for
permission every time the dual licensing changes.

**Q: I only contribute docs or issues. Is a CLA needed?** The CLA covers
Contributions submitted (including code, docs, and review discussion).
